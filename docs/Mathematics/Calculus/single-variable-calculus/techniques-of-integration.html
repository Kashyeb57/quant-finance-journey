Chapter 7: Techniques of Integration

7.1 Integration by Parts​

∫u dv=uv−∫v du\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du∫udv=uv−∫vdu

(Derived from product rule: d(uv)=u dv+v dud(uv) = u \, dv + v \, dud(uv)=udv+vdu)

CHOOSING u and dv — use "LIATE" priority for u:

  • L →\to→ Logarithms
  • I →\to→ Inverse trig
  • A →\to→ Algebraic (polynomials)
  • T →\to→ Trigonometric
  • E →\to→ Exponential

Example: ∫x⋅ex dx\int x \cdot e^x \, dx∫x⋅exdx

u=x  ⟹  du=dxdv=ex dx  ⟹  v=ex=x⋅ex−∫ex dx=x⋅ex−ex+C=ex(x−1)+C\begin{aligned} u &= x \implies du = dx \\ dv &= e^x \, dx \implies v = e^x \\ &= x \cdot e^x - \int e^x \, dx \\ &= x \cdot e^x - e^x + C \\ &= e^x(x - 1) + C \end{aligned}udv​=x⟹du=dx=exdx⟹v=ex=x⋅ex−∫exdx=x⋅ex−ex+C=ex(x−1)+C​

7.4 Partial Fractions​

Decompose rational functions into simpler fractions:

For rational functions P(x)Q(x)\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}Q(x)P(x)​ where deg(P)<deg(Q)\text{deg}(P) < \text{deg}(Q)deg(P)<deg(Q):

CASE 1: Q(x)Q(x)Q(x) has distinct linear factors

1(x−1)(x+2)=Ax−1+Bx+2\frac{1}{(x-1)(x+2)} = \frac{A}{x-1} + \frac{B}{x+2}(x−1)(x+2)1​=x−1A​+x+2B​

(Multiply both sides by denominator, solve for A and B)

CASE 2: Q(x)Q(x)Q(x) has repeated linear factors

1(x−1)2=Ax−1+B(x−1)2\frac{1}{(x-1)^2} = \frac{A}{x-1} + \frac{B}{(x-1)^2}(x−1)21​=x−1A​+(x−1)2B​

CASE 3: Q(x)Q(x)Q(x) has irreducible quadratic factors

1(x2+1)(x−1)=Ax+Bx2+1+Cx−1\frac{1}{(x^2+1)(x-1)} = \frac{Ax+B}{x^2+1} + \frac{C}{x-1}(x2+1)(x−1)1​=x2+1Ax+B​+x−1C​

7.7 Approximate Integration​

MIDPOINT RULE (rectangles at midpoints):

∫abf(x)dx≈Δx[fˉ(x1)+fˉ(x2)+⋯+fˉ(xn)]\int_a^b f(x) dx \approx \Delta x [\bar{f}(x_1) + \bar{f}(x_2) + \dots + \bar{f}(x_n)]∫ab​f(x)dx≈Δx[fˉ​(x1​)+fˉ​(x2​)+⋯+fˉ​(xn​)]

TRAPEZOIDAL RULE (trapezoids):

∫abf(x)dx≈Δx2[f(x0)+2f(x1)+⋯+2f(xn−1)+f(xn)]\int_a^b f(x) dx \approx \frac{\Delta x}{2} [f(x_0) + 2f(x_1) + \dots + 2f(x_{n-1}) + f(x_n)]∫ab​f(x)dx≈2Δx​[f(x0​)+2f(x1​)+⋯+2f(xn−1​)+f(xn​)]

SIMPSON'S RULE (parabolas — most accurate):

∫abf(x)dx≈Δx3[f(x0)+4f(x1)+2f(x2)+4f(x3)+⋯+f(xn)]\int_a^b f(x) dx \approx \frac{\Delta x}{3} [f(x_0) + 4f(x_1) + 2f(x_2) + 4f(x_3) + \dots + f(x_n)]∫ab​f(x)dx≈3Δx​[f(x0​)+4f(x1​)+2f(x2​)+4f(x3​)+⋯+f(xn​)]

7.8 Improper Integrals​

Type 1: Infinite limits

∫a∞f(x) dx=lim⁡t→∞∫atf(x) dx\int_a^\infty f(x)\,dx = \lim_{t\to\infty} \int_a^t f(x)\,dx∫a∞​f(x)dx=t→∞lim​∫at​f(x)dx

But ∫1∞1x dx=∞\displaystyle\int_1^\infty \frac{1}{x}\,dx = \infty∫1∞​x1​dx=∞ — this one diverges, "just barely."
