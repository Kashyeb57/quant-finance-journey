3.2 Product and Quotient Rules​

Product Rule:

ddx[f(x)⋅g(x)]=f′(x)⋅g(x)+f(x)⋅g′(x)\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) \cdot g(x)] = f'(x) \cdot g(x) + f(x) \cdot g'(x)dxd​[f(x)⋅g(x)]=f′(x)⋅g(x)+f(x)⋅g′(x)

Memory Trick: "first times derivative of second plus second times derivative of first"

Example:

ddx[x2⋅sin⁡(x)]=2x⋅sin⁡(x)+x2⋅cos⁡(x)\frac{d}{dx}[x^2 \cdot \sin(x)] = 2x \cdot \sin(x) + x^2 \cdot \cos(x)dxd​[x2⋅sin(x)]=2x⋅sin(x)+x2⋅cos(x)

Quotient Rule:

ddx[f(x)g(x)]=f′(x)⋅g(x)−f(x)⋅g′(x)[g(x)]2\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right] = \frac{f'(x) \cdot g(x) - f(x) \cdot g'(x)}{[g(x)]^2}dxd​[g(x)f(x)​]=[g(x)]2f′(x)⋅g(x)−f(x)⋅g′(x)​

Memory Trick: "low d-high minus high d-low, over low squared"

Example:

ddx[sin⁡(x)x]=cos⁡(x)⋅x−sin⁡(x)⋅1x2=xcos⁡(x)−sin⁡(x)x2\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{\sin(x)}{x}\right] = \frac{\cos(x) \cdot x - \sin(x) \cdot 1}{x^2} = \frac{x \cos(x) - \sin(x)}{x^2}dxd​[xsin(x)​]=x2cos(x)⋅x−sin(x)⋅1​=x2xcos(x)−sin(x)​

3.4 The Chain Rule​

The most important rule in calculus.

If y=f(u)y = f(u)y=f(u) and u=g(x)u = g(x)u=g(x), then:

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=f′(g(x))⋅g′(x)\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx} = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)dxdy​=dudy​⋅dxdu​=f′(g(x))⋅g′(x)

VISUAL — think of it as gears: x→gu→fyx \xrightarrow{g} u \xrightarrow{f} yxg​uf​y

Rate of change of yyy w.r.t xxx = (rate yyy changes per uuu) ×\times× (rate uuu changes per xxx)

Examples:

ddx[sin⁡(x2)]=cos⁡(x2)⋅2xddx[e3x]=e3x⋅3ddx[(x2+1)5]=5(x2+1)4⋅2x\begin{aligned} \frac{d}{dx}[\sin(x^2)] &= \cos(x^2) \cdot 2x \\ \frac{d}{dx}[e^{3x}] &= e^{3x} \cdot 3 \\ \frac{d}{dx}[(x^2+1)^5] &= 5(x^2+1)^4 \cdot 2x \end{aligned}dxd​[sin(x2)]dxd​[e3x]dxd​[(x2+1)5]​=cos(x2)⋅2x=e3x⋅3=5(x2+1)4⋅2x​

Nested chain rule:

ddx[sin⁡(ex2)]=cos⁡(ex2)⋅ex2⋅2x\frac{d}{dx}[\sin(e^{x^2})] = \cos(e^{x^2}) \cdot e^{x^2} \cdot 2xdxd​[sin(ex2)]=cos(ex2)⋅ex2⋅2x

Quant Finance: Chain rule is THE fundamental tool in derivatives pricing. Itô's lemma (stochastic chain rule) derives Black-Scholes PDE.

3.5 Implicit Differentiation​

Sometimes y is not solved explicitly for x. Differentiate both sides and treat y as y(x):

Example: x2+y2=25x^2 + y^2 = 25x2+y2=25 (circle of radius 5)

Implicit curve: x² + y² = 25

Differentiate both sides:

2x+2y dydx=0⟹dydx=−xy2x + 2y\,\frac{dy}{dx} = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{x}{y}2x+2ydxdy​=0⟹dxdy​=−yx​

At point (3,4)(3,4)(3,4): slope =−34= -\tfrac{3}{4}=−43​.

Quant Finance: Extracting implied volatility from option prices; yield curve bootstrapping.

Cost C(x) and marginal cost C'(x)

Natural Growth/Decay:

dydt=ky  ⟹  y(t)=y0⋅ekt\frac{dy}{dt} = ky \quad \implies \quad y(t) = y_0 \cdot e^{kt}dtdy​=ky⟹y(t)=y0​⋅ekt

3.10 Linearization and Differentials​

Linear approximation (tangent line approximation):

L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)L(x) = f(a) + f'(a)(x - a)L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)
Linearization: L(x) tangent to f(x) at x = a

L(x)≈f(x)L(x) \approx f(x)L(x)≈f(x) near x=ax = ax=a.

Differential:

dy=f′(x)dxdy = f'(x) dxdy=f′(x)dx

When xxx changes by dxdxdx, yyy changes by approximately dydydy.
