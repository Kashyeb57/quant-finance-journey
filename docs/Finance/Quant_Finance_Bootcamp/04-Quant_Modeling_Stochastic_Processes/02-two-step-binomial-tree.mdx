## Two step binomial tree

Each step has length **Δt**. Each upward branch has factor **u** and probability
**p**; each downward branch has factor **d** and probability **1 − p**.

Two arrows converge on the middle node — that is the point of the diagram. It
is reached by **up then down** or **down then up**, and both land in the same
place because:

$$
S_0ud=S_0du.
$$

The tree contains these stock prices and option values:

| Time | Node | Stock price | Option value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Initial | S₀ | f |
| Δt | Up | S₀u | f_u |
| Δt | Down | S₀d | f_d |
| 2Δt | Up-up | S₀u² | f_uu |
| 2Δt | Up-down / down-up | S₀ud | f_ud |
| 2Δt | Down-down | S₀d² | f_dd |

## Mathematical calculation

The one-step valuation formula, now using the length of **one** step, is:

$$
f=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_u+(1-p)f_d\right].
$$

The risk-neutral up probability is:

$$
p=\frac{e^{r\Delta t}-d}{u-d}.
$$

At the **up node**:

$$
f_u=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_{uu}+(1-p)f_{ud}\right].
$$

At the **down node**:

$$
f_d=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_{ud}+(1-p)f_{dd}\right].
$$

The handwritten margin highlights the two weighted terms at that down node:

$$
f_{ud}\,p,\qquad f_{dd}\,(1-p).
$$

Bring the two intermediate values back to today:

$$
f=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_u+(1-p)f_d\right].
$$

Substituting the two intermediate expressions gives the boxed two-step result:

$$
\boxed{
f=e^{-2r\Delta t}\left[p^2f_{uu}+2p(1-p)f_{ud}+(1-p)^2f_{dd}\right]
}.
$$

The two paths to the middle node account for the **2p(1 − p)** term.

## Notation and volatility

| Symbol | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| u | Upward movement |
| d | Downward movement |
| p | Probability of upward movement |
| 1 − p | Probability of downward movement |
| σ | Volatility |

The volatility diagram distinguishes **historical volatility** and **implied
volatility**:

```text
                       σ → Volatility
                         /         \
          Historical volatility    Implied volatility
                    |                      ↑
                    ↓                      |
          BT → Option value        Option value in the market
```

The historical-volatility branch feeds the **binomial tree (BT)** to produce
an **option value**. The other branch connects **option value in the market**
back to **implied volatility**.
