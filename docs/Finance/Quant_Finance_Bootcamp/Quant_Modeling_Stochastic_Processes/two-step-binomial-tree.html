Two Step Binomial Tree & Mathematical Calculation

Two step binomial tree​

Each step has length Δt. Each upward branch has factor u and probability p; each downward branch has factor d and probability 1 − p.

Two arrows converge on the middle node — that is the point of the diagram. It is reached by up then down or down then up, and both land in the same place because:

S0ud=S0du.S_0ud=S_0du.S0​ud=S0​du.

The tree contains these stock prices and option values:

TimeNodeStock priceOption value
0InitialS₀f
ΔtUpS₀uf_u
ΔtDownS₀df_d
2ΔtUp-upS₀u²f_uu
2ΔtUp-down / down-upS₀udf_ud
2ΔtDown-downS₀d²f_dd

Mathematical calculation​

The one-step valuation formula, now using the length of one step, is:

f=e−rΔt[pfu+(1−p)fd].f=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_u+(1-p)f_d\right].f=e−rΔt[pfu​+(1−p)fd​].

The risk-neutral up probability is:

p=erΔt−du−d.p=\frac{e^{r\Delta t}-d}{u-d}.p=u−derΔt−d​.

At the up node:

fu=e−rΔt[pfuu+(1−p)fud].f_u=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_{uu}+(1-p)f_{ud}\right].fu​=e−rΔt[pfuu​+(1−p)fud​].

At the down node:

fd=e−rΔt[pfud+(1−p)fdd].f_d=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_{ud}+(1-p)f_{dd}\right].fd​=e−rΔt[pfud​+(1−p)fdd​].

The handwritten margin highlights the two weighted terms at that down node:

fud p,fdd (1−p).f_{ud}\,p,\qquad f_{dd}\,(1-p).fud​p,fdd​(1−p).

Bring the two intermediate values back to today:

f=e−rΔt[pfu+(1−p)fd].f=e^{-r\Delta t}\left[p f_u+(1-p)f_d\right].f=e−rΔt[pfu​+(1−p)fd​].

Substituting the two intermediate expressions gives the boxed two-step result:

f=e−2rΔt[p2fuu+2p(1−p)fud+(1−p)2fdd].\boxed{ f=e^{-2r\Delta t}\left[p^2f_{uu}+2p(1-p)f_{ud}+(1-p)^2f_{dd}\right] }.f=e−2rΔt[p2fuu​+2p(1−p)fud​+(1−p)2fdd​]​.

The two paths to the middle node account for the 2p(1 − p) term.

Notation and volatility​

SymbolMeaning
uUpward movement
dDownward movement
pProbability of upward movement
1 − pProbability of downward movement
σVolatility

The volatility diagram distinguishes historical volatility and implied volatility:

                       σ → Volatility
                         /         \
          Historical volatility    Implied volatility
                    |                      ↑
                    ↓                      |
          BT → Option value        Option value in the market

The historical-volatility branch feeds the binomial tree (BT) to produce an option value. The other branch connects option value in the market back to implied volatility.
