4. Shape & Position

Seeing the whole distribution and locating values within it: histograms and density, percentiles and quartiles (Value-at-Risk), and the 5-number summary with its outlier fences.

📄️ Histograms

Central tendency and dispersion each compress a dataset to a single number. A histogram shows you the whole shape at once — it's the fastest way to see whether data is symmetric, skewed, or hiding fat tails.

📄️ Percentiles and Quartiles

Central tendency and dispersion summarize a distribution; percentiles locate a specific value within it. They're how you say "this observation is high" or "this is a bad day" precisely, without assuming any particular distribution shape.

📄️ The 5-Number Summary

Five percentiles capture the entire skeleton of a distribution — its center, its spread, and its extremes — in one compact summary. It's the basis of the box plot and of a beautifully simple, distribution-free way to flag outliers.
